## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intimate, serene digital flagship for artisanal, high-end perfumery on mobile devices. The philosophy rejects frantic urgency, garish promotional badges, and visual noise, instead cultivating the deliberate pacing of an exclusive boutique atelier. 

The aesthetic is anchored in warm minimalism:
- **Quiet Architectural Restraint:** Clean, proportional grids and modernist type scales convey authority without coldness.
- **Warm Tactile Nuance:** Creamy, sun-warmed surfaces replace clinical laboratory whites, evoking natural paper stocks, stone plinths, and hand-blown glass.
- **Sensory Pacing:** Intentional negative space and distraction-free discovery simulate the deliberate act of sampling fine fragrance notes.

## Colors

The palette draws strictly from elemental earth, raw parchment, and warm ceramic tones.

- **Primary Canvas (`#FFFBE9`):** A soft, creamy warm ivory base that softens optical fatigue and establishes an organic tactile canvas.
- **Surface & Structural Containers (`#E3CAA5`):** Soft warm sand/beige used for structural trays, product card backdrops, elevated drawers, and sensory dividers.
- **Primary Accent (`#B17457`):** Rich warm amber-terracotta reserved for focused primary touchpoints, conversion CTAs, active states, and discrete discovery highlights.
- **Secondary Tone (`#AD8B73`):** Muted warm taupe for supporting elements, secondary toggles, subtle badges, and understated borders.
- **Neutral Highlight (`#CEAB93`):** Warm dusty almond utilized for delicate card tints, hovered states, and fragrance pyramid containers.
- **Primary Text (`#4A4947`):** Deep warm charcoal/espresso for crisp, legible typographic hierarchy with reduced harshness compared to pure black.
- **Secondary Text (`rgba(74, 73, 71, 0.7)`):** Muted espresso for fragrance notes, subtitles, metadata, and legal text.

## Typography

The typographic system pairs an architectural modernist sans-serif for display titles with a calibrated, highly legible neutral grotesk for dense product details and body prose.

- **Display & Headings:** Rendered with expansive letter-spacing in uppercase or measured title case. Evokes bespoke fragrance labelling and boutique storefront signage.
- **Body & Captions:** Tuned for comfortable optical rhythm on small viewports. Provides editorial clarity when outlining olfactory notes (top, heart, base notes) and ingredient provenance.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** High tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) and all-caps styling provide clarity for product capacities (e.g., `50 ML / 1.7 FL. OZ.`), batch codes, and metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first 4-column fluid layout that expands to 6 columns on tablet breakpoints. The rhythm emphasizes vertical breathing room between olfactory storytelling segments.

- **Canvas Margins:** Fixed at `1.25rem` (20px) on mobile viewports to provide a secure gutter while maximizing card surface area.
- **Grid Gutters:** Maintained at `1rem` (16px) for dual-column product arrangements, tightening to `0.75rem` (12px) for thumbnail carousels.
- **Content Blocks:** Narrative modules (e.g., perfumer interviews, accord breakdowns) enforce a minimum vertical space of `space-xl` (40px) to simulate editorial spreads.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids simulated light sources, drop shadows, and heavy skeuomorphic bevels. Spatial hierarchy is communicated through tonal layering and hair-thin, architectural framing:

- **Surface Tiers:** Baseline content rests on `#FFFBE9`. Secondary elevations, contextual overlays, and product pedestals adopt `#E3CAA5` or `#CEAB93`.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Visual divisions rely on 1px borders tinted in `#AD8B73` at 25% to 40% opacity (`rgba(173, 139, 115, 0.3)`).
- **Modal Sheets & Overlays:** Slide-up cart panels and drawer navigation utilize a subtle backdrop veil of `rgba(74, 73, 71, 0.35)` with zero blur, directing focus through tonal contrast.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a sharp geometric language (`roundedness: 0`). 

- Rectilinear forms, precise 90-degree corners, and crisp planar edges evoke glass perfume bottles, heavy paper packaging boxes, and modernist architecture.
- Structural borders across cards, buttons, badges, and input fields remain strictly unrounded to preserve high-fashion editorial rigor.

## Components

### Header
- Fixed top navigation bar at 56px height on `#FFFBE9`.
- Clean minimal layout: Left drawer menu toggle, centered architectural wordmark in uppercase, right icon group (discrete search toggle and cart bag trigger).
- Cart icon features a micro-badge: `#B17457` solid square pip containing count in `label-sm` inverted ivory text.
- Bottom border: 1px solid `rgba(173, 139, 115, 0.25)`.

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Rectangular, background `#B17457`, text `#FFFBE9`, zero border radius. Text styled with `label-lg`, uppercase, tracking `0.1em`. Generous vertical padding (`0.875rem`).
- **Secondary / Ghost CTA:** Background transparent, 1px solid `#4A4947`, text `#4A4947`. On active touch, fills with `#E3CAA5` at 50% opacity.
- **Text CTA:** Minimalist underline text with a 1px offset, rendered in `label-md` tracking.

### Product Cards
- Contained within 1px solid `rgba(173, 139, 115, 0.25)` borders.
- Background surface `#FFFBE9` or soft sand `#E3CAA5`.
- Image presentation: 1:1.25 ratio portrait ratio showcasing bottle silhouettes against calm empty spaces.
- Typography: Product title in `headline-sm`, olfactory profile (e.g., "Santal, Cardamom, Iris") in `body-sm` (`rgba(74, 73, 71, 0.7)`), and price displayed in `label-lg` using `#4A4947`.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Understated styling: Single bottom border (1px solid `#AD8B73`) or fully enclosed sharp-bordered field.
- Background: Pure `#FFFBE9` with placeholder text in secondary muted charcoal.
- Active focus state: Bottom border shifts to solid 1.5px `#B17457` without glowing rings.

### Chips & Olfactory Tags
- Sharp, rectangular containers with 1px border `rgba(173, 139, 115, 0.35)`.
- Internal padding: `0.375rem` top/bottom, `0.75rem` left/right.
- Active selection state: Solid `#B17457` background with `#FFFBE9` text.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp square indicators (checkbox) and nested square frames (radio) instead of circles.
- Inactive: 1px border `#AD8B73` with `#FFFBE9` center.
- Selected: Solid `#B17457` fill or a solid inner `#4A4947` square block.

### Olfactory Accord Accordion
- Clean dividing lines separating "Top Notes", "Heart Notes", and "Base Notes".
- Expandable trigger rows with clean minimalist `+` / `−` indicators in `#4A4947`.

### Shared Footer
- Warm anchored background in `#E3CAA5`.
- Top section: Minimal single-field newsletter signup ("Join the Archive") with integrated submit arrow.
- Middle section: Single-column clean links stack (`label-md`, uppercase) covering Return Policy, Terms of Service, Privacy, and FAQ.
- Bottom section: Discreet copyright notice and provenance line in `body-sm` muted charcoal.
